Questions, answered

Good to know before you start.

What is the difference between the two plans?

Daily Boost covers one newly uploaded post per day. Full Profile covers multiple posts per day and includes latest plus older posts, according to the plan's supported coverage.

What is included in the plans?

Listed engagement options include likes, comments, shares, saves, views, plays, watchtime, story views, story likes, profile visits, reposts and GIF comments. Exact availability may depend on content type and current system support.

How much do the yearly plans save?

Daily Boost costs ₹18,000 per year versus ₹26,640 for 12 monthly payments, a difference of ₹8,640. Full Profile costs ₹25,000 per year versus ₹50,400 for 12 monthly payments, a difference of ₹25,400.

Can you guarantee more followers, reach or viral results?

No. Social platform distribution and audience response are not fully controllable. InfluensorOS does not guarantee followers, reach, virality or specific engagement outcomes.

How do I start or ask about a plan?

Message InfluensorOS through its Instagram profile. The team can confirm current availability, plan terms, account eligibility and setup steps before you purchase.
